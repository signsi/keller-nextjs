# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Procurement and engineering contacts at industrial manufacturing companies (machine building, medtech, electronics, automotive, aviation) in the Deutschschweiz who need galvanic or chemical surface-treatment for their components (plating, electropolishing, hard chrome, powder coating, etc.). They arrive technically informed, comparing suppliers on precision, reliability, and certifications, and want a fast, low-friction way to describe a part and get a qualified answer or quote.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and lead-generation website for Keller Galvanik AG, a Swiss surface-treatment company with 40+ years of experience. The site establishes technical credibility (procedures, industries served, quality/certification standards) and converts visitors into inquiry/quote requests. Success is a visitor who trusts the company's expertise within seconds and submits an inquiry (or calls) without friction.

## Brand Personality

Precise, serious, technical, but not cold or corporate. Swiss-industrial rigor in structure and detail, not sterile bureaucracy. A named human (Peter Keller, Geschäftsführer) stands behind every trust moment, this is a real person answering, not a ticketing system.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS form: rounded shadow card, floating labels, a plain blue "Submit" button, indistinguishable from any startup signup form.
- Cold, bureaucratic government-form feel: sterile fields with no sense of who's on the other end or what happens next.
- Overloaded stock-industrial imagery: decorative factory photos, gear icons, or clichéd "industry" visuals standing in for genuine technical detail.

## Design Principles

1. Show technical credibility over generic reassurance, back claims with concrete process, spec, or certification detail rather than vague adjectives.
2. Put a named human at every trust moment, the inquiry experience should feel like reaching Peter Keller directly, not submitting a ticket into a void.
3. Precision without coldness, Swiss-industrial rigor in structure and typography, never sterile or bureaucratic.
4. Respect the visitor's time, the quote request should feel like the fastest, clearest path to an answer, not a generic multi-field form to slog through.
5. Let real craft carry the visual weight, no decorative stock-industry imagery standing in for genuine detail or content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. No additional documented user needs.
